# Fanpage Grounded Content Guidelines

Use this skill with fanpage content, research, content calendar, caption, script, image brief, and post-review tasks when the output should avoid generic "AI-like" writing and instead feel grounded in real-world evidence.

This skill is brand-neutral. Combine it with the page/brand skill and domain skill for the current page.

## Core Rule

Every meaningful post needs a grounding anchor.

A grounding anchor is a concrete detail that ties the post to reality: a real company, real project, real news item, source, local situation, customer scene, weather/season, operational constraint, technical case, regulation, market observation, or documented example.

Do not invent grounding anchors. If the brief does not provide them and the topic needs them, ask for details or request research.

## Acceptable Grounding Anchors

Use at least one of these when writing a post:

- Real company, brand, project, building, public case, or market example with source.
- User-provided company/project/customer situation, clearly treated as user-provided.
- A specific engineering, business, or operational scenario that is plausible and framed as illustrative, not as a fake real case.
- Current news, public report, law, standard, weather, event, or market change with source.
- Local context: city, district, store area, construction condition, traffic, heat/rain/season, event timing, or audience behavior.
- Real workflow detail: file handoff, revision loop, design stage, site coordination issue, menu/operation bottleneck, staffing pattern, or delivery constraint.
- Numeric data only when sourced or user-provided.

## Banned Grounding

Do not:

- Invent a company name and present it as a real case.
- Invent a project name, client, engineer, owner, quotation, revenue, savings, delay, accident, legal outcome, or technical result.
- Write "a company A" as if it is factual when no such case was provided.
- Claim a standard/code says something specific without source verification.
- Claim current weather, market trend, price, regulation, or news without checking or being given the data.
- Use fake "people said" quotes.
- Use vague phrases as a substitute for evidence: "many companies", "recently", "the market shows", "experts say", "in real projects" unless supported or clearly framed.

## How To Use Company Or Case Examples

When a real company/case is known:

- Name it only if it is public, sourced, and safe to mention.
- Include source notes outside the publish copy when the claim depends on research.
- Keep the lesson relevant to the page's audience.
- Do not copy competitor wording.
- Do not imply the brand worked on that project unless confirmed.

When the user provides a private/internal example:

- Use it as "user-provided" context.
- Anonymize if confidentiality is unclear.
- Avoid naming the company/project unless the user explicitly permits it.

When no real case is available:

- Use an illustrative scenario, not a fake case.
- Say "ví dụ trong một mặt bằng 20-30m2..." or "trong một hồ sơ BIM cuối giai đoạn APD..." instead of pretending a named company had the issue.
- Prefer asking for a real example if the user expects a case study.

## Required Pre-Writing Check

Before writing, identify:

```text
GROUNDING CHECK
- Real anchor available:
- Source/user-provided/internal:
- What can be safely said:
- What must not be claimed:
- Need research? yes/no
```

Do not include this check in the final publish copy unless the user asks for process notes. Use it to control the writing.

## When To Trigger Research

Require research or source links when the post uses:

- Current news, trend, price, law, regulation, standard, policy, weather, market movement, or statistics.
- Company/case examples not provided by the user.
- Legal, technical, medical, financial, construction-safety, ESG, environmental, or compliance claims.
- Competitor, public project, or named brand analysis.

If research tooling is unavailable, write a safer generic educational post and clearly mark source-dependent details as missing.

## Writing Pattern

Use this pattern for grounded posts:

1. Start with a concrete scene, event, question, or real-world constraint.
2. Explain why it matters to the audience.
3. Connect it to the page's domain or offer.
4. Add a practical lesson, checklist, or decision point.
5. Add CTA/footer/hashtags from the brand skill.
6. Add source notes after publish copy when sources support the post.

## Page-Specific Guidance

### Technical / Construction Pages

Prefer anchors such as:

- A public building/project case.
- A known standard or code topic with source.
- A drawing/detailing problem.
- A site coordination issue.
- A weather or construction-season constraint.
- A real design stage such as concept, basic design, APD/APS, shop drawing, construction documentation, or site revision.

Example direction:

- Weak: "Sàn phẳng giúp tối ưu không gian."
- Stronger: "Trong các mặt bằng có tầng hầm hoặc tầng thương mại nhỏ, hệ dầm có thể làm cao độ trần bị chia cắt khi đi thêm ống gió, sprinkler và máng cáp. Vì vậy, nhiều nhóm thiết kế phải so sánh sàn phẳng với hệ dầm ngay từ bước concept."

### Franchise / F&B Pages

Prefer anchors such as:

- Store size, rent pressure, staffing, delivery flow, food court/kiosk setting, menu complexity, prep time, or peak-hour queue.
- Public F&B market example with source.
- User-provided franchise lead question or store situation.
- Season/weather/holiday/customer behavior.

Example direction:

- Weak: "Kinh doanh F&B cần chọn mô hình phù hợp."
- Stronger: "Một mặt bằng 18-25m2 ở khu dân cư không vận hành giống quán 80m2. Nếu bê nguyên mô hình bếp, menu và nhân sự của quán lớn vào kiosk nhỏ, chi phí cố định có thể vượt quá khả năng chịu của doanh thu ban đầu."

### Outsourcing / B2B Service Pages

Prefer anchors such as:

- Real workflow stage, file handoff, revision bottleneck, deadline pressure, production backlog, naming convention, coordination issue, or deliverable type.
- Public market/company example with source.
- User-provided client scenario.

Example direction:

- Weak: "Outsourcing BIM giúp tiết kiệm thời gian."
- Stronger: "Khi một bureau d'etudes nhận thêm hồ sơ vào cuối giai đoạn APD, phần cập nhật mặt bằng, coupe và chi tiết Revit thường không khó về ý tưởng nhưng rất tốn giờ production. Outsourcing chỉ hiệu quả nếu brief, naming rule và revision flow được chốt rõ từ đầu."

## Final Quality Checklist

Before returning content, verify:

- Does the post include at least one concrete grounding anchor?
- Is the anchor real, sourced, user-provided, or clearly illustrative?
- Did the post avoid invented companies, projects, people, quotes, statistics, and outcomes?
- Are source notes included for source-dependent claims?
- Does the post sound like a real page admin with domain experience, not a generic marketing paragraph?
- Is the grounding connected to the audience's decision, pain point, or daily work?
- Are brand footer, hashtag, CTA, and watermark rules preserved through the page-specific skill?
