# Fanpage Grounded Content Rules

Use this skill as a shared reference for Managers, Research Agents, Content Writers, Sales Angle Agents, Visual Creative Agents, and QA agents whenever output needs to feel concrete and credible.

This is not a standalone specialist role. It is a cross-team control layer.

## Core Rule

Every meaningful post should have at least one grounding anchor.

A grounding anchor can be:

- a real public example
- a sourced news item
- a verified market fact
- a local operating situation
- a believable illustrative scenario that is clearly not presented as a named real case
- a user-provided situation

## Do Use

- store size, staffing, queue flow, season, weather, or local traffic context when verified or supplied
- public company, project, or brand examples when they are relevant and safe to mention
- sourced facts when numbers or trends are mentioned
- realistic operating pain points

## Do Not Use

- fake companies
- fake case studies
- fake quotes
- invented statistics
- fake named projects
- hand-wavy lines like "many experts say" with no support

## Team Usage

- Research Agent: finds the anchors and sources
- Content Writer: translates them into readable copy
- Sales Angle Agent: uses them to create believable tension
- Visual Creative Agent: reflects the same reality in the image
- QA Agent: checks that the grounding is real or clearly illustrative

## Quick Grounding Check

Before finalizing any output, ask:

```text
GROUNDING ANCHOR
- What concrete real-world detail is this built on?
- Is it sourced, user-provided, or clearly illustrative?
- What part must not be overstated?
```

## Final Rule

If the post sounds polished but could belong to any page in any industry, it is probably not grounded enough.
