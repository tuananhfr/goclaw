# Brand UBOT Vietnam Guidelines

Use this skill for any task involving UBOT VIỆT NAM - Sàn phẳng không dầm & Tối ưu chi phí, Vietnamese Facebook content, Ubot/U-Bot Beton solution posts, flat slab lead generation, project case posts, cost-optimization messages, or image briefs under this page.

Also apply:

- `facebook-fanpage-baseline-rules` for Facebook structure.
- `fanpage-grounded-content-rules` for real cases, real project situations, evidence, and non-generic content.
- `ubot-vietnam-floor-system-knowledge` for technical floor-system knowledge and claim safety.

## Page Basics

- Page name: UBOT VIỆT NAM - Sàn phẳng không dầm & Tối ưu chi phí.
- Language: Vietnamese.
- Page direction: technical solution + lead generation.
- Main solution: sàn phẳng không dầm Ubot / U-Bot Beton.
- Main promise direction: technical benefits, cost optimization, structural efficiency, long-span use, open space, and practical project application.
- Main audience: chủ đầu tư, nhà thầu, kỹ sư, kiến trúc sư, quản lý dự án, and owners of high-rise, commercial, industrial, villa, and townhouse projects.
- Watermark safe zone: top left.

This page may educate, but it is not a neutral engineering encyclopedia. It should guide readers toward consulting UBOT/LPC for a project-specific floor solution.

## Page Voice

Use a tone that is:

- Technical, direct, and solution-oriented.
- More lead-generation than academic.
- Confident enough for marketing, but careful with engineering and cost claims.
- Practical and grounded in project situations.
- Suitable for both technical readers and decision makers.

Avoid:

- Generic motivational construction copy.
- Overly academic explanations with no CTA.
- Unsupported absolute claims about savings, span, safety, ESG, compliance, or suitability.
- Fake case studies or invented project details.

## Content Pillars

Use these routes:

- Ubot/U-Bot Beton solution introduction.
- Cost optimization: structural cost, steel, concrete, dead load, formwork, ceiling, MEP coordination, and long-term efficiency.
- Technical benefits: flat ceiling, no beams, long spans, lighter slab strategy, open space, MEP flexibility, acoustic/thermal discussion when supported.
- Project applications: high-rise, commercial, industrial, villa, townhouse, open-plan spaces, large-span rooms, and MEP-heavy projects.
- Real cases and project situations: villa span 9m, open ceiling needs, large floor plates, or anonymized project constraints.
- Green/European technology positioning: European technology, recycled material, material efficiency, and environmental direction with cautious wording.
- Comparison content: Ubot vs traditional beam-slab, flat slab alternatives, hollow/voided slab logic, and global floor system lessons.

## Claim Rules

The page may discuss these claim themes:

- giảm chi phí kết cấu
- giảm tải trọng công trình
- giảm thép / giảm bê tông / giảm vật liệu
- vượt nhịp lớn
- trần phẳng không dầm
- tăng thẩm mỹ
- hỗ trợ không gian MEP
- thân thiện môi trường

But write them cautiously:

- Use `có thể`, `trong một số điều kiện`, `tùy mặt bằng, nhịp, tải trọng, phương án kết cấu và kiểm toán kỹ thuật`.
- Do not imply Ubot is best for every project.
- Do not claim final savings without project-specific calculation.
- Do not use exact numbers such as `giảm đến 15% chi phí kết cấu` or `giảm đến 30% lượng thép` unless the user provides a case, source, calculation basis, or confirms it as approved page claim.

When the user provides a number, label it as user-provided or case-based if needed. When no basis is provided, rewrite as a softer directional claim.

## Post Format

For normal solution/lead posts:

```text
[Strong technical/business hook]

[Concrete project situation or pain point]

[How Ubot may address the issue]

[Caution or decision factor if technical]

[CTA]

[Fixed footer]

[Hashtags]
```

Rules:

- Keep normal Facebook post copy under 400 words unless the user asks for long-form.
- Use concrete project context whenever possible.
- Use icons moderately for scanability.
- Do not show labels like AIDA, PAS, Problem, Solution, or CTA in publish-ready copy.
- Add caveat for technical/cost claims.

## Visual Rules

For image prompts, briefs, or QA:

- Watermark is top left. Keep that zone clean.
- Do not place headline, price/saving number, CTA, QR code, drawing label, or key slab detail in the top-left watermark zone.
- Prefer technical solution visuals: Ubot module, voided slab section, flat slab section, construction site, slab reinforcement, BIM/model view, before/after beam vs flat ceiling, or infographic comparison.
- Visuals may be marketing-forward but must remain technically credible.
- Use real project images only when permission/source is clear.
- Keep on-image text readable on mobile.
- Avoid unrelated logos or fake project identifiers.
