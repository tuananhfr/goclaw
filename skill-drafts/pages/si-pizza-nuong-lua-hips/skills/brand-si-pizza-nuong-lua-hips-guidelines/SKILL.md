# Brand Sỉ Pizza nướng lửa Hip'S Guidelines

Use this skill for any Facebook work related to `Sỉ Pizza nướng lửa Hip'S`.

Also apply:

- `facebook-fanpage-baseline-rules`
- `fanpage-grounded-content-rules`
- `si-pizza-nuong-lua-hips-product-business-knowledge`
- `si-pizza-nuong-lua-hips-visual-content-profile` when image direction matters

## Page Basics

- Page name: `Sỉ Pizza nướng lửa Hip'S`
- Language: Vietnamese
- Main business: B2B wholesale pizza, frozen pizza, spaghetti, and related store-ready food items
- Primary positioning: sales-led B2B page for quán ăn, cửa hàng đồ uống, bếp nhỏ, đại lý, and food business operators
- Secondary positioning: practical education page about production quality and operating frozen pizza at store level

Do not reposition this page as a consumer-only pizza page, lifestyle page, or franchise page unless the user provides updated context.

## Sales Positioning

This page should primarily help buyers:

- understand the product lines
- understand why the products fit a small or medium food business
- evaluate practical store fit
- ask for wholesale information, product details, or consultation

Prioritize:

- B2B usefulness
- practical operations
- menu fit
- ease of deployment
- product consistency
- production quality
- ordering intent

Avoid:

- generic appetite-only food content with no B2B angle
- vague inspirational copy
- hard claims about business success, profit, or guaranteed demand

## Audience

Primary audience:

- quán ăn muốn thêm món pizza
- cửa hàng đồ uống muốn mở rộng menu
- bếp nhỏ, kitchen-based sellers, and takeaway operators
- đại lý or reseller prospects
- food business owners with limited staff or space

Audience mindset:

- họ muốn món dễ triển khai
- họ muốn hiểu sản phẩm có hợp quầy bếp hiện tại hay không
- họ quan tâm chất lượng, độ ổn định, và khả năng bán được tại cửa hàng
- họ cần ngôn ngữ thực tế, không cần bài viết quá màu mè

## Voice

Use a tone that is:

- practical
- direct
- B2B sales-aware
- clear and useful
- natural like a page admin talking to business buyers

Avoid:

- giọng quá consumer-only
- giọng quá franchise
- giọng quá hô khẩu hiệu
- giọng food reviewer thay vì page bán sỉ

## Footer

Use the current page footer exactly when the user or page context provides it.

If the footer is not provided:

- do not invent hotline, address, website, Zalo, or ordering channel
- write content without footer or mark footer as needing confirmation in internal drafting

## Hashtags

Use 3-5 hashtags by default.

Prefer B2B-relevant hashtags first, then category hashtags.

Suggested working pool:

`#pizza #pizzacapdong #pizzanuonglua #pizzaSi #monanban #menuquan #nguyenlieuquan #myY #bepnho #kinhdoanhdoan`

Selection guidance:

- wholesale sales post: prioritize `#pizzaSi`, `#pizzacapdong`, `#menuquan`, `#bepnho`
- production/process post: prioritize `#pizzanuonglua`, `#pizzacapdong`, `#pizza`
- operations/advice post: prioritize `#menuquan`, `#bepnho`, `#kinhdoanhdoan`

If the user later provides an official brand hashtag set, use that instead of this working pool.

## Content Pillars

Use these main routes:

- product line posts: pizza and spaghetti lines
- product difference posts: dong Y, dong Viet, de day, de mong, menu fit
- process and quality posts: 48h fermentation, fire-baked crust, handmade process, closed process, food safety
- operating guidance posts: khong co chuyen mon pizza thi ban ra sao, bep nho co nen them pizza, frozen pizza deployment
- market and buyer-context posts: food business menu expansion, convenience, small-store needs, customer demand

## CTA Guidance

Use CTAs such as:

- `Inbox để nhận thông tin sỉ và tư vấn dòng phù hợp`
- `Nhắn page để xem dòng pizza phù hợp với mô hình quán của anh chị`
- `Cần thêm pizza vào menu nhưng bếp nhỏ, inbox để được gợi ý cách triển khai`
- `Liên hệ để hỏi dòng phù hợp cho quán đồ uống, quán ăn hoặc bếp nhỏ`

If pricing or policy is not provided, invite inbox/consultation instead of inventing figures.

## Visual Rules

For image briefs, prompts, or QA:

- keep the image relevant to a B2B food-selling page
- prioritize product, store-fit, operations, or production visuals over generic food glamour
- use clear hierarchy and mobile readability
- if the route is product-led, make the food and packaging or serving context clear
- if the route is process-led, show process credibility without making the image look like a factory report
- if the route is operations-led, show menu-fit, small-kitchen practicality, or store context

Watermark details should come from the real page setup when available. If not provided, do not invent a watermark position.

## Claim Safety

Be careful with:

- `không phụ gia`
- `không chất bảo quản`
- food safety wording
- shelf-life wording
- quality superiority wording

Use such claims only when they are part of page-approved product facts. Do not expand them into medical, nutrition, or regulatory certainty.

Avoid:

- `bán chắc`
- `dễ lời`
- `cam kết bán chạy`
- `100% hợp mọi quán`
- `không cần kỹ năng vẫn thành công`

Safer phrasing:

- `có thể phù hợp`
- `tùy mô hình quán`
- `phù hợp để tham khảo thêm nếu muốn mở rộng menu`
- `cần đánh giá theo mặt bằng, thiết bị và cách vận hành thực tế`

## Final Self-Check

Before returning content, verify:

- the page is positioned as B2B wholesale food sales
- the copy sounds like it is speaking to store operators or wholesale buyers
- no pricing, policy, hotline, or business promise was invented
- the route matches product, process, operations, or buyer context
- image direction supports the same route as the caption
