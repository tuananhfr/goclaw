# Sỉ Pizza nướng lửa Hip'S Product Business Knowledge

Use this skill when the task involves product explanation, product comparison, production-process content, B2B buyer education, small-store operating advice, spaghetti posts, or frozen pizza business-fit content for `Sỉ Pizza nướng lửa Hip'S`.

Also apply:

- `brand-si-pizza-nuong-lua-hips-guidelines`
- `fanpage-grounded-content-rules`
- `fanpage-research-agent-guidelines` when current market, food safety, or regulation claims need sources

## Role

This skill defines what the page sells and what kind of operational or menu-fit content it should publish.

It covers:

- pizza product lines
- spaghetti items
- production and quality talking points
- practical operating questions from buyers
- safe framing for B2B sales content

## Known Facts And Unknown Facts

Known page-approved facts include only the product lines, flavors, and process points listed in this skill.

Do not invent or assume:

- price list
- minimum order quantity
- delivery area
- shipping method
- storage temperature
- shelf life
- reheating or baking instructions
- packaging specs
- wholesale policy
- reseller margin
- certificates or inspection documents
- production capacity
- exact ingredient percentages

If the content needs any of these details, either avoid the detail or mark it as needing confirmation from the Manager.

## Product Lines

### 1. Pizza Lines

#### Dòng Ý

Pizza flavors:

- bò bằm
- ba chỉ hun khói
- xúc xích
- hải sản
- 4 vị phô mai

Key characteristics:

- nướng lửa
- không men nở
- không phụ gia
- không chất bảo quản
- ủ men tự nhiên 48h
- nướng lửa chuẩn vị Ý

Use carefully:

- If using the claims `không phụ gia` or `không chất bảo quản`, keep them as page-approved product facts only.
- Do not turn them into health, nutrition, or regulatory superiority claims.

#### Dòng Việt

Pizza flavors:

- thập cẩm
- bò ngô
- xúc xích ngô

Key characteristics:

- đế dày
- có thêm sữa
- điều chỉnh để phù hợp khẩu vị Việt

Use angle:

- menu-friendly
- easier for buyers targeting broader Vietnamese taste preference
- suitable for practical store selling rather than gourmet framing

## Product Selection Logic

Use Dòng Ý when the angle needs:

- fire-baked crust
- Italian-style positioning
- natural 48h fermentation
- flavor variety such as bò bằm, ba chỉ hun khói, xúc xích, hải sản, 4 vị phô mai

Use Dòng Việt when the angle needs:

- thicker base
- Vietnamese taste adaptation
- broader easy-selling menu fit
- flavors such as thập cẩm, bò ngô, xúc xích ngô

Use Mỳ Ý when the angle needs:

- combo support
- menu completion
- add-on item for shops already selling pizza or drinks
- cross-sell with pizza

If the brief does not specify a line, write generally about menu-fit or ask only when the product line affects accuracy.

## Main Business Angles

This page is not only about taste. It is about:

- helping stores add pizza or spaghetti to menu
- helping small kitchens sell pizza without needing a full pizza-specialist team
- helping buyers understand product difference and deployment fit

## Production And Quality Content

Use these talking points when the brief is about process or quality:

- ủ men tự nhiên 48h
- đế nướng lửa
- thủ công hoàn toàn
- quy trình khép kín
- an toàn vệ sinh thực phẩm

Rules:

- do not invent certifications
- do not invent factory-scale specs
- do not make legal or regulatory compliance claims unless the user provides them
- explain process in a buyer-useful way, not like a technical audit report

## Operations And Store-Fit Content

Use this route for questions such as:

- không có chuyên môn pizza thì bán như thế nào
- quán đồ ăn bếp nhỏ có nên bán pizza không
- frozen pizza có phù hợp để thêm vào menu không
- cửa hàng đồ uống có nên thêm pizza để tăng món bán không

Useful angles:

- menu expansion without rebuilding the whole kitchen
- operational simplicity
- store-fit by space and staff
- practical buyer concern rather than abstract business theory

Do not promise:

- bán chắc
- doanh thu chắc chắn tăng
- lợi nhuận đảm bảo
- ai cũng làm được như nhau

Safer framing:

- `có thể phù hợp nếu quán muốn mở rộng menu`
- `tùy mặt bằng, thiết bị, và cách vận hành`
- `phù hợp để tìm hiểu thêm nếu bếp hiện tại còn hạn chế nhân sự hoặc không gian`

- connect product features to operational meaning
- use real store situations whenever possible
- avoid overexplaining food culture if it does not help the buyer decide
- keep product and business logic connected
