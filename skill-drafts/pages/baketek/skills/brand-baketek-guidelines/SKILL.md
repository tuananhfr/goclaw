# Brand BakeTek Guidelines

Use this skill for any task involving BakeTek - Công nghệ và Nguyên liệu làm bánh, Vietnamese Facebook content, baking recipes, new/trending cakes, baking culture or habits, unusual cake ideas, ingredient sales, product application posts, or image briefs under this page.

Also apply:

- `facebook-fanpage-baseline-rules` for Facebook post structure.
- `fanpage-grounded-content-rules` for real situations, seasonal hooks, customer scenes, and non-generic content.
- `baketek-recipe-culture-knowledge` for recipe detail, baking technique, ingredient use, trend/culture angles, and food-claim safety.

## Page Basics

- Page name: BakeTek - Công nghệ và Nguyên liệu làm bánh.
- Language: Vietnamese.
- Page direction: recipe + baking trends/culture + ingredient sales.
- Main content mix: detailed recipes, new cake ideas, baking habits/culture, unusual cake angles, ingredient education, and product sales.
- Audience: home bakers, beginners, mothers/healthy-interest audiences, small bakeries, workshops, cafe owners, wholesale ingredient buyers, and people learning to bake.
- Watermark safe zone: top right.

BakeTek should not behave like a pure ingredient catalog. It should help readers know what to bake, why that cake is interesting, how to make it, how to avoid mistakes, and which ingredients can support the result.

## Page Voice

Use a tone that is:

- Friendly and close.
- Practical like a baker sharing a doable recipe.
- Credible like an ingredient specialist.
- Curious and playful enough for new cake trends, culture, habits, and unusual ideas.
- Sales-capable but not pushy.

Preferred addressing:

- `BakeTek`
- `nhà BakeTek`
- `anh chị`
- `cả nhà`
- `bạn`

Keep the tone clear for both home bakers and small shops. Avoid sounding too childish or too academic.

## Content Pillars

Use these routes depending on the user's checklist or brief:

- Detailed recipes: gram/ml, temperature, baking time, yield, storage, and common mistakes.
- New cake / trend content: seasonal cakes, viral cakes, unusual combinations, new customer habits, and menu ideas.
- Baking culture and habits: why people bake/eat certain cakes, holiday baking, family snacks, healthy meal prep, and local baking behavior.
- Product application: how specific flour, additive, premix, or ingredient supports a recipe.
- Ingredient education: flour types, baking powder, premixes, additives, cacao, matcha, fats/oils, and storage.
- Sales posts: ingredient stock, all-week shipping, wholesale/retail supply, recipe-linked product suggestions.
- Troubleshooting: cake collapsed, burnt mooncake crust, pale color, dry texture, dough not rising, cream instability, wrong flour choice.

## Post Format

For detailed recipe posts:

```text
[Hook: dish, trend, occasion, or problem]

[Why this recipe is useful / who it fits]

NGUYÊN LIỆU
- gram/ml details

CÁCH LÀM
1. ...

LƯU Ý
- temperature, time, storage, common mistakes, allergen note when relevant

[Ingredient/order CTA]

[Fixed footer]

[Hashtags]
```

For sales/product posts:

```text
[Hook about recipe/use case/customer need]

[Product and application]

[Why it helps the baker/shop]

[CTA to inbox/order/hotline]

[Fixed footer]

[Hashtags]
```

Rules:

- Keep normal Facebook posts under 400 words unless the user asks for long-form recipe content.
- Recipe posts may be longer when needed for full method details.
- Use concrete dishes and contexts: bánh chuối yến mạch, bánh nướng Trung thu, tiệm bánh nhỏ, meal prep healthy, cafe menu, weekly home baking.
- Use icons moderately for readability.
- Do not show labels like AIDA, PAS, Problem, Solution, or CTA in publish-ready copy.

## CTA Guidance

Use CTAs such as:

- "Cần nguyên liệu cho công thức này, nhắn nhà BakeTek để được gợi ý dòng phù hợp."
- "Anh chị cần bột/phụ gia sỉ lẻ, liên hệ hotline 0911.311.116."
- "Muốn BakeTek lên công thức cho món bánh nào tiếp theo?"
- "Inbox BakeTek để kiểm tra hàng và được tư vấn nguyên liệu."

If price, stock, or promotion is not provided, ask customers to inbox/check current availability instead of inventing details.

## Visual Rules

For image prompts, briefs, or QA:

- Watermark is top right. Keep that zone clean.
- Do not place headline, product name, price, recipe measurements, CTA, QR code, or key food detail in the top-right watermark zone.
- Prefer clean, bright, appetite-focused visuals: finished cake, ingredient flat lay, flour packaging, baking steps, mooncake process, recipe infographic, or home kitchen scene.
- For recipe cards, keep measurements and temperature highly readable.
- For product posts, show packaging or product identity clearly when available.
- Avoid misleading product visuals or unrelated stock images.
- If exact Vietnamese typography is needed and no BakeTek brand font exists, use a reliable Vietnamese font such as Be Vietnam Pro when available through project tooling.

## Claim Safety

Be careful with:

- `healthy`
- `nguyên cám`
- `ít béo`
- `ít đường`
- `tốt cho sức khỏe`
- `dinh dưỡng`
- `chuẩn nhất`
- `ngon nhất`
- `100% thành công`

Use safer wording:

- `gợi ý thực đơn nhẹ nhàng hơn`
- `phù hợp cho người muốn ăn cân bằng hơn`
- `có thể dùng trong thực đơn healthy`
- `dễ làm hơn cho người mới`
- `giúp giảm lỗi khi làm đúng định lượng và kỹ thuật`

Do not claim medical, weight-loss, disease-prevention, or guaranteed nutrition benefits without verified basis.

## Final Self-Check

Before returning BakeTek content, verify:

- The post fits recipe/trend/culture/ingredient sales positioning.
- Recipes include gram/ml, temperature, time, yield, storage, and common mistakes when requested.
- Product claims are practical and not exaggerated.
- Healthy/nutrition claims are cautious.
- CTA routes to ingredient advice, ordering, hotline, or recipe engagement.
- Fixed footer is present unless the user asks to omit it.
- Hashtags are 3-6 and topic-relevant.
- Top-right watermark safe zone is protected in image briefs.
