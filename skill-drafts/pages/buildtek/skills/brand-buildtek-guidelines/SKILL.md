## Page Basics

- Page name: `Buildtek`.
- Language: Vietnamese.
- Page type: service-led home and living page.
- Main business: home renovation, turnkey house construction, architecture and interior direction for private houses.
- Main audience: homeowners, families planning to build, people planning to renovate, and people looking for architectural or interior inspiration.
- Watermark safe zone: use the current page watermark config when available. If unavailable, keep the top right clean by default.

If official footer, hotline, website, address, brand hashtag set, or watermark position is not provided, do not invent them.

## Positioning

Buildtek is not just a page that posts beautiful houses.
It is also not a page that only pushes services in a dry way.

Buildtek content should balance:

- inspiration
- practicality
- process clarity
- trust in execution
- service inquiry potential

Main objectives:

- help viewers imagine a better living space
- explain renovation and house-building decisions more clearly
- build trust in Buildtek's architecture and construction thinking
- create demand for consultation, concept discussion, renovation planning, or turnkey service inquiry

Avoid:

- generic lifestyle home content with no service logic
- empty aesthetic praise
- hard-sell service copy with no useful value
- overclaiming around construction process, speed, cost, or quality

## Voice

Use a tone that is:

- professional
- practical
- clear
- warm
- design-aware
- trustworthy

Brand feeling should be:

- understands real living needs
- has good taste
- explains clearly
- feels like a real construction and design team

Avoid:

- over-poetic lifestyle writing
- overly technical construction jargon
- exaggerated luxury tone
- aggressive sales language

- families planning to build a private house
- people looking for architecture and interior inspiration

## Claim Safety

Do not invent:

- cost
- timeline
- structural specs
- legal scope
- material specifications
- years of experience
- project counts
- client names
- before/after proof
- guaranteed no-extra-cost or no-delay claims

Safer wording:

- `co the phu hop`
- `tuy hien trang cong trinh`
- `tuy nhu cau su dung`
- `tuy ngan sach va cach trien khai`
- `can duoc danh gia cu the`

## Final Self-Check

- the post feels useful, not generic
- the route is clear
- the content supports trust in design or execution
- the CTA fits consultation intent
- no unsupported service, technical, timeline, or cost claim was introduced
- footer and hashtags are handled safely
