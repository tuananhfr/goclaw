# Buildtek Visual Content Profile

Use this skill as the page-specific visual profile for `Buildtek`.

This is mainly used by:

- `Page Manager`
- `Visual Creative Agent`
- `Content Writer Agent`
- `Brand QA / Claim Safety Agent`

The Page Manager owns this profile and passes the relevant part into each task packet.
This is a default map for the page, not a hard lock for every future post.

## Core Principle

Buildtek may use AI renders and AI concept visuals.

But the visuals must still feel:

- modern
- realistic
- clean
- intentional
- suitable for real houses and real living

Avoid generic AI house glamour with no practical design logic.

## Global Visual Direction

- tone: modern, clean, architectural, calm
- visual mood: high-taste but still livable
- prioritize believable space and proportion
- use light, material, and layout to communicate quality
- avoid visual clutter
- avoid cheap luxury look
- avoid impossible architecture, distorted furniture, or awkward circulation logic

## Visual Route Mapping

### 1. Service Introduction Posts

Use visual mode:

- `Story Or Experience Visual`
- or `Sales Explainer Visual`

Style:

- aspirational but real
- clean and premium
- home-living focused

Subject focus:

- family house exterior
- interior living spaces
- renovation transformation mood
- architecture and function together

Text density:

- low to medium

On-image text:

- 1 short headline
- optionally 1 short service cue

### 2. Pain-Point Posts

Use visual mode:

- `News Or Insight Card`
- or `Comparison Explainer`

Style:

- problem-aware
- practical
- easy to scan

Subject focus:

- tight layout
- dark room
- inconvenient circulation
- cluttered storage
- renovation stress points

Text density:

- medium

On-image text:

- 1 pain-point-led headline
- 2-4 short support points or comparison cues

### 3. Process / Workflow Posts

Use visual mode:

- `Educational Infographic`

Style:

- clear
- structured
- step-based

Subject focus:

- renovation steps
- turnkey workflow
- implementation phases
- what to prepare before starting

Text density:

- medium

On-image text:

- 1 headline
- 2-5 short step cues

Avoid:

- giant text blocks
- too many tiny details

### 4. Style / Inspiration Posts

Use visual mode:

- `Comparison Visual`
- or `Story Or Experience Visual`

Style:

- aesthetic
- clearly differentiated
- tasteful and contemporary

Subject focus:

- architecture style comparison
- interior style comparison
- material mood
- living atmosphere

Text density:

- low to medium

On-image text:

- 1 short comparison headline
- optionally 2 short labels

### 5. Tips / Practical Advice Posts

Use visual mode:

- `Educational Infographic`
- or `Comparison Explainer`

Style:

- useful
- clean
- homeowner-friendly

Subject focus:

- do / don't
- wrong vs better layout logic
- practical living improvement

Text density:

- medium

On-image text:

- 1 useful headline
- 2-4 short support points

### 6. Case / Concept / Showcase Posts

Use visual mode:

- `Concept Showcase`

Style:

- elegant
- realistic
- portfolio-like

Subject focus:

- facade
- living room
- kitchen
- bedroom
- renovation concept

Text density:

- low

On-image text:

- 1 short concept headline at most

## AI Render Rules

AI render is allowed for this page.

Requirements:

- architecture must feel plausible
- layout must feel usable
- furniture scale must feel believable
- lighting should feel natural or intentionally designed
- materials should not look plastic or random

Avoid:

- impossible houses
- distorted windows, stairs, or structure
- overly glossy fake luxury
- chaotic decorative clutter
- AI humans with visible errors

## Infographic Rules

Use infographic when the content is:

- process-oriented
- step-based
- mistake-oriented
- comparison-oriented

Requirements:

- clean layout
- strong hierarchy
- easy mobile reading
- not too much text

## Text On Image

- not every image needs text
- use text only when it helps clarify the route
- service and concept visuals may stay lighter
- process and comparison visuals may carry more text
- do not overload the image with long CTA, footer, or too many bullets

## CTA Treatment On Image

- prefer CTA in the caption
- use very light CTA on image only when it truly helps

## Watermark And Safe Zones

Follow the brand skill and current watermark config.

Fallback rule:

- keep the default top-right zone clean if the real watermark config is unavailable

## Quality Filters

Before approving a Buildtek visual, verify:

- the space looks believable
- the route is obvious at a glance
- the visual feels modern and design-aware
- the text amount fits the route
- the image does not look like generic AI slop
- the image supports real living logic, not just surface beauty

## Final Rule

For Buildtek, a strong visual should make viewers feel both:

- "this looks good"
- "this could actually work for real living"
