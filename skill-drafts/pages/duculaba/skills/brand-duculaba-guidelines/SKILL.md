# Brand Duculaba Guidelines

Use this skill for any task involving Duculaba - Thế giới đồ làm bánh, Vietnamese Facebook content, baking ingredient sales posts, product introductions, baking ingredient applications, simple recipe-support content, or image briefs under the Duculaba page.

Also apply the general `facebook-fanpage-content-guidelines` skill when available. For product/application knowledge, flour, additives, premixes, recipe notes, or ingredient comparison content, also apply `duculaba-baking-supply-knowledge` when available. For posts that need real examples, market observations, weather/season, or customer situations, also apply `fanpage-grounded-content-guidelines`.

## Page Basics

- Page name: Duculaba - Thế giới đồ làm bánh.
- Language: Vietnamese.
- Main business: baking flour and baking ingredients, with signs of both wholesale and retail sales.
- Current positioning: sales-led page with light ingredient application guidance.
- Main audience: small bakeries, home-based baking businesses, small workshops, cake shops, drink/cafe owners that need baking ingredients, wholesale buyers, and serious home bakers.
- Secondary audience: casual home bakers and people learning to bake.
- Watermark safe zone: top left.

Do not reposition Duculaba as a cooking school, recipe-only page, or equipment store unless the user provides updated context.

## Sales Positioning

Duculaba content should primarily help customers choose and buy ingredients.

Prioritize:

- Product availability.
- Product use case.
- Fit for bakery/workshop needs.
- Wholesale/retail ordering.
- Cost practicality without making absolute cheapest/best claims.
- Simple baking application notes.
- Real customer situations such as small bakery production, batch baking, cafe menu items, or home-baking demand.

Avoid:

- Overly abstract brand storytelling.
- Recipe content that forgets product sales.
- Making every post sound like a generic cooking blog.
- Claiming premium/standard/best quality without a clear basis.

## Voice And Addressing

Use a tone that is:

- Friendly, direct, practical, and sales-oriented.
- Warm enough for home bakers but still useful for small shops and wholesale buyers.
- Clear about product use and ordering.
- Not overly cute, not too formal, and not technical beyond what buyers need.

Preferred addressing:

- `Duculaba`
- `nhà Duculaba`
- `anh chị`
- `quý khách`
- `khách sỉ/lẻ`

Use `em` only when the post is written in a direct shop-admin sales voice. Keep it natural and not excessive.

Selection guidance:

- General product post: use `#duculaba #nguyenlieulambanh #botmi`.
- Tips/application post: add `#meovat` or `#moingaylammotmonngon`.

## Content Pillars

Use these routes:

- Product introduction: flour, wheat flour lines, premixes, additives, butter/oil substitutes, cacao, matcha, flavoring, corn starch, and baking ingredients.
- Product application: which cake/bread/drink/bakery use case fits the ingredient.
- Wholesale/retail sales: stock availability, batch needs, shop/workshop supply, quantity, price inquiry, and direct ordering.
- Practical buyer advice: how to choose flour, ingredient storage, common use cases, cost-conscious substitution, and handling notes.
- Light recipe support: simple formula ideas only when measurements and steps are provided or framed as suggested use, not as an unverified full recipe.
- Trend/product occasion: holidays, busy bakery seasons, cafe menu demand, hot weather storage issues, or seasonal cake demand when provided or verified.

Do not over-focus on course/class content, tools/equipment, or full recipe education unless the user provides new direction.

## Post Format

For normal sales posts:

```text
[Hook about product, customer need, stock, or use case]

[What the product is / who it fits]

[Practical application or buyer reason]

[CTA to inbox/order/contact]

[Fixed footer]

[Hashtags]
```

Rules:

- Keep normal Facebook post copy under 400 words unless the user asks for long-form.
- Use short paragraphs and mobile-readable lines.
- Mention use cases concretely: bánh mì, bánh ngọt, bánh bao, bánh bông lan, cookies, topping, đồ uống, xưởng bánh, tiệm bánh, hoặc mẻ làm tại nhà.
- Add a grounded buyer situation when possible, not generic sales claims.
- Use icons moderately when they make the post easier to scan.
- Do not show labels like AIDA, PAS, Problem, Solution, or CTA in publish-ready copy.

## CTA Guidance

Use CTAs such as:

- "Anh chị cần bột/nguyên liệu số lượng sỉ lẻ, inbox Duculaba để được tư vấn."
- "Nhắn nhà Duculaba để kiểm tra hàng và giá hôm nay."
- "Cần chọn bột theo món bánh đang làm, gửi Duculaba tên món để được gợi ý dòng phù hợp."
- "Liên hệ Phone/Zalo để lên đơn nhanh."

If price is not provided, ask customers to inbox/check current price instead of inventing price.

## Visual Rules

For image prompts, briefs, or QA:

- Watermark is top left. Keep that area visually clean.
- Do not place headline, price, product name, CTA, QR code, small text, or important package detail in the top-left watermark zone.
- Prefer product images with clear packaging/label when selling a specific product.
- For application posts, show appetizing but realistic bakery results and keep the ingredient/product connection clear.
- Use clean, bright, practical baking-supply visuals: ingredient bags, flour texture, bakery counter, dough, bread/cake output, or simple infographic.
- Avoid unrelated stock images that hide the actual product.
- Keep text readable on mobile.
- If exact Vietnamese typography is needed and no Duculaba brand font exists, use a reliable Vietnamese font such as Be Vietnam Pro when available through project tooling.

## Claim Safety

Avoid absolute claims unless verified:

- `rẻ nhất`
- `ngon nhất`
- `tốt nhất`
- `chuẩn nhất`
- `100% thành công`
- health/nutrition claims without evidence
- guaranteed baking result

Allowed safer wording:

- `giá tốt`
- `phù hợp cho`
- `dễ ứng dụng`
- `lựa chọn kinh tế cho tiệm/xưởng`
- `được nhiều khách chọn`
- `có thể dùng cho`

## Final Self-Check

Before returning Duculaba content, verify:

- The post is Vietnamese and fits a baking ingredient sales page.
- Product/use case is concrete, not generic.
- CTA routes to inbox/contact/order.
- Fixed footer is present unless the user asks to omit it.
- Hashtags fit the product/topic and start with `#duculaba`.
- Top-left watermark safe zone is preserved in image briefs.
- No unsupported absolute quality, price, health, nutrition, or guaranteed-result claim is introduced.
