# LPC France Visual Content Profile

Use this skill as the page-specific visual profile for `LPC France`.

This is mainly used by:

- `Page Manager`
- `Visual Creative Agent`
- `Content Writer Agent`
- `Brand QA / Claim Safety Agent`

The Page Manager owns this profile and passes the relevant part into each task packet.
This is a default map for the page, not a hard lock for every future post.

## Core Principle

LPC France may use 100% research AI visuals.

But the images must still feel:

- professional
- modern
- clean
- realistic
- technically credible
- appropriate for the European AEC market

Do not let the page look like:

- a cheap AI architecture page
- a flashy B2C ad page
- a random infographic page
- a fantasy building render feed

## Global Visual Direction

- tone: high-end B2B, architectural, technical, clean
- palette: neutral, professional, often blue/gray/white or restrained corporate tones
- composition: clean hierarchy, modern whitespace, structured layout
- realism matters more than visual spectacle
- avoid fake-looking humans, warped structures, meaningless blueprint text, and impossible engineering geometry
- avoid over-decorated icons and loud promo graphics

## Research AI Rules

This page is allowed to use 100% research AI visuals.

That means:

- AI architectural renders are acceptable
- AI BIM-like model visualizations are acceptable
- AI structural frame concepts are acceptable
- AI office collaboration scenes are acceptable
- AI infographic support is acceptable

But:

- buildings must still look plausible
- structures must still look realistic
- perspectives must still feel professional
- avoid fake software UI with nonsense details
- avoid distorted people, hands, faces, and impossible models

## Text On Image

- use short on-image text only
- French-English bilingual overlay is allowed when needed
- do not overload the image with long bilingual paragraphs
- prefer short terms such as:
  - `BIM Support`
  - `Modelisation 3D / 3D Modeling`
  - `Support Structure / Structural Support`
  - `Production Capacity`
  - `From brief to output`
  - `Support for AEC teams`

## Watermark And Safe Zones

Follow the brand skill and current watermark config.

Fallback rule:

- keep the top-left zone clean if runtime watermark config is unavailable

Do not place these in the watermark zone:

- headline
- CTA
- small technical text
- drawing details
- project identifiers
- QR codes

## Quality Filters

Before approving an LPC France visual, verify:

- the image looks suitable for a European B2B AEC page
- the architecture or engineering feel is obvious
- the route is clear at a glance
- the text amount fits the route
- the image does not look cheap, fake, or over-generated
- no fake proof, fake project, or fake certification is implied visually
- watermark safe zones remain protected

## Final Rule

For LPC France, a good image should feel like a clean, high-end, technically credible AEC outsourcing brand visual, not just a generic AI architecture render.
