# Facebook Guardrails Common

Use this skill as a compact boundary layer.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- do not invent unsupported facts
- do not invent price, policy, guarantee, or proof
- do not silently change locked content outside your scope
- do not create final images before content approval when the workflow requires content first
- do not manually draw logo, watermark, hotline, or address on image
- do not publish or pretend to publish without explicit approval from the lead or user

## Final Rule

Use this skill as a fence, not a template.
The lead may add stricter page-specific rules in the task brief.
