# Facebook Guardrails Common

Use this skill as a compact shared guardrail layer.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- do not invent unsupported facts
- do not invent price, policy, guarantee, or proof
- do not publish without `POST_NOW`
- do not create final images before content approval
- do not change `CONTENT_LOCKED` inside image execution
- do not manually draw logo, watermark, hotline, or address on image

## Final Rule

Use this skill as a fence, not a template.
